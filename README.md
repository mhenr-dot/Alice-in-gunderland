Description of our project:
Our project is a top down, shooter video game loosely based off of the characters and settings of Alice in Wonderland where you must travel through levels and slay enemies. It involves the main character you play, Alice, going into "Gunderland" where the world of Alice in Wonderland is after you. While in Gunderland, the world throws many enemies at you that you must dodge and defeat. You are given a "card blaster" and collect other special cards that change up your style of shooting enemies and go through a series of levels. Each level carries their own set of enemies as well as mini bosses that you must attempt to defeat. At the end you go to defeat the main boss, The Queen of Hearts and escape Gunderland. Our project aims to solve the problem that there has not yet been a video game based off of Alice in Wonderland that is action packed and challenging. It also aims to solve boredom as this game is meant to entertain people.
The top 3 tracks we aim to achieve is Beginner, Most on Theme, and Game Hack.
